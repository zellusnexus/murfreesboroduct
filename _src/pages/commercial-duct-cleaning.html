Duct Cleaning for Rutherford County Businesses

Commercial HVAC systems move far more air than a house, often for 10 to 24 hours a day. Dust, debris, and odors build up quickly, and the people breathing that air are your staff and your customers. Murfreesboro Duct Cleaning provides air duct cleaning for small and mid-sized commercial properties throughout Murfreesboro, Smyrna, La Vergne, and the rest of Rutherford County.

Our Commercial Cleaning Process

  1. Site walk. We review your rooftop or split systems, duct layout, and access points, then provide a written quote.
  2. Scheduling. We set a time that keeps disruption to a minimum, including after hours.
  3. Containment. Work areas are protected and registers sealed while the system is under negative pressure.
  4. Source removal. HEPA-filtered vacuum equipment and mechanical agitation remove debris from supply and return ductwork.
  5. Documentation. You receive photos and a summary of what was cleaned and anything we noticed that needs attention.

Why Commercial Duct Cleaning Matters

  • Healthier workplace. Less dust and fewer airborne allergens circulating through shared spaces.
  • Better customer experience. No dusty vents above the sales floor or musty smell in the waiting room.
  • Airflow problems solved. Clogged returns and supply runs are a common cause of hot and cold spots in offices.
  • After a build-out. Tenant improvements fill ducts with drywall and construction dust. See post-construction duct cleaning.

Commercial Duct Cleaning FAQs

Can you clean our ducts after business hours?

Yes. Evening, early-morning, and weekend appointments are available so cleaning doesn't interfere with customers or staff.

How long does commercial duct cleaning take?

It depends on the size of the space and the number of systems. A small office or retail suite can often be done in a single visit. Larger buildings may be cleaned in zones over several visits.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. We can provide itemized quotes, photo documentation, and scheduling coordination for property managers and landlords with multiple units or buildings.

Do you clean restaurant kitchen hoods?

No. Kitchen exhaust hood and grease duct cleaning is a separate fire-code service. We clean the HVAC supply and return ductwork in restaurants and dining rooms.
