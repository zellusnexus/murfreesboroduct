Construction Dust Doesn't Stay in the Work Zone

Sanding drywall produces an extremely fine powder that travels everywhere. If the heat or air ran at any point during your project, and it almost always does, that dust was pulled into your return ducts and spread through the whole system. Sawdust, insulation fibers, bits of wire, and even screws and drywall scraps commonly end up inside open registers.

Once your remodel is done, that debris keeps blowing out of the vents for months. It settles on new floors, coats fresh paint, and clogs your filter far faster than normal.

When You Need Post-Construction Duct Cleaning

  • Kitchen, bathroom, or basement remodels
  • Room additions and bonus-room build-outs
  • Flooring replacement, especially sanded hardwood
  • Drywall repair or ceiling replacement
  • Newly built homes where the HVAC ran during construction
  • Commercial tenant build-outs and office renovations
  • Smoke or soot cleanup after a small fire

Rutherford County has been one of the fastest-growing areas in Tennessee, and new subdivisions keep going up in Blackman, Rockvale, Christiana, Smyrna, and around Murfreesboro. Builders often run the HVAC system to dry out drywall and keep crews comfortable, which pulls construction dust straight into a brand-new duct system. If you've just moved into a new build and you're already seeing dust around your vents, a post-construction cleaning is one of the best things you can do for your home's air.

How We Clean After Construction

  1. Inspect the debris. We check registers, the return plenum, and branch runs for drywall dust and loose debris.
  2. Remove large debris by hand. Screws, drywall chunks, and scraps are pulled from boots and register openings.
  3. Negative-pressure cleaning. Our HEPA-filtered vacuum and agitation tools clear the fine dust from every run.
  4. Return and plenum focus. The return side usually holds the most construction dust, so it gets extra attention.
  5. Filter recommendation. We'll recommend changing your filter after cleaning and again a few weeks later.

Tips for Your Next Project

Planning a remodel? Ask your contractor to cover supply registers and returns in the work area, turn the system off during heavy sanding, and use a cheap filter during construction that gets replaced at the end. Then call us once the dust settles.

Post-Construction FAQs

How soon after a remodel should I have my ducts cleaned?

Once all sanding, painting, and flooring work is complete. Cleaning before the dust-producing work is finished means the ducts will just get dirty again.

Should a brand-new home need duct cleaning?

Often, yes. If the HVAC system was used during construction, the ducts likely contain drywall dust and debris. We'll inspect and show you before recommending a cleaning.

Can builders or contractors schedule this for their clients?
