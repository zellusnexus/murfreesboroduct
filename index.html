Warning Signs

Do Your Ducts Need Cleaning?

Most homes should have their air ducts cleaned every 3–5 years. Call us if you notice any of these signs:

Read the full guide to warning signs →

  • Visible dust blowing out of vents
  • Allergies or sinus issues that worsen indoors
  • Musty or stale odors when the air kicks on
  • Recent renovation or construction
  • You just moved into a new home
  • Pets that shed in the house
  • Rising energy bills with no explanation
  • Rooms that never get as warm or cool as others

FAQ

Air Duct Cleaning FAQs

How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?

Most homes benefit from duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Homes with pets, smokers, allergy sufferers, or recent renovations may need it more often.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

A typical home takes about 2 to 4 hours depending on its size, the number of vents, and the condition of the ductwork.

Will duct cleaning make a mess in my home?

No. We seal off vents and use a negative-pressure system so debris is pulled out of your home, not into it. We use floor protection and clean up when we're done.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Yes, an adult should be home to let us in and walk through the results with our technician at the end of the job.

How much does duct cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your home and the number of vents and duct runs. Call (615) 857-5923 for a free, upfront quote with no hidden fees.

Do you clean commercial buildings?

Yes. We provide duct cleaning for offices, retail stores, restaurants, and other commercial properties throughout Murfreesboro.
