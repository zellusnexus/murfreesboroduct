About Murfreesboro Duct Cleaning

Local, Focused, and Honest

Murfreesboro Duct Cleaning is based at 925 S Church St in Murfreesboro. We do one thing, air duct cleaning, and we try to do it better than anyone else in Rutherford County. We're not a national franchise call center or an HVAC company looking to sell you a new system. When you call, you talk to people who live and work here.

What We Stand For

  • Upfront pricing. You'll know your price before any work begins. No $49 teaser rates and no surprise upsells.
  • Doing the job right. A full source-removal cleaning of every supply and return with professional HEPA-filtered negative-pressure equipment, not a quick pass with a shop vac.
  • Showing our work. Before & after photos on every job, so you can see exactly what came out.
  • Respect for your home. Trained, background-checked technicians who use floor protection and clean up after themselves.
  • Honest recommendations. If your ducts don't need cleaning, we'll tell you.

Who We Serve

We clean ducts for homeowners, renters (with landlord approval), landlords, property managers, builders, and businesses across Rutherford County, from downtown Murfreesboro to Smyrna, La Vergne, Blackman, Rockvale, and the rural communities to the south and east.

Our Guarantee

We stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right, call us and we'll make it right.
