About the Service

What does air duct cleaning include?

A whole-home cleaning includes every supply duct and register, every return duct and grille, the supply trunk lines, and the return plenum. We use HEPA-filtered negative-pressure equipment with rotary brushes and air whips, clean the vent covers, and show you before & after photos.

How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?

Most homes benefit from duct cleaning every 3 to 5 years. Homes with pets, smokers, allergy sufferers, or recent renovations may need it more often.

How long does air duct cleaning take?

A typical home takes about 2 to 4 hours depending on its size, the number of vents, and the condition of the ductwork. Homes with two HVAC systems take longer.

Will duct cleaning make a mess in my home?

No. We seal off vents and use a negative-pressure system so debris is pulled out of your home, not into it. We use floor protection and clean up when we're done.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Yes, an adult should be home to let us in and walk through the results with our technician at the end of the job.

Do I need to move furniture?

Just make sure we can reach each vent and return. If a heavy piece of furniture blocks a register, let us know when you book and we'll help.

Do you clean ducts in attics and crawl spaces?

Yes. Many Rutherford County homes have ducts in a crawl space or attic, and we clean them. We'll tell you if we find a damaged or disconnected duct.

Pricing

How much does duct cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your home and the number of vents, returns, and HVAC systems. Call (615) 857-5923 for a free, upfront quote with no hidden fees, or read our air duct cleaning cost guide for typical local ranges.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Quotes are free and we confirm your price before any work begins.

Why are some companies advertising $49 duct cleaning?

Very low "whole house" prices usually lead to upsells, hidden per-vent charges, or a quick vacuum of the register openings instead of a real cleaning. Always ask what the total price includes before you book.

Health & Home

Will duct cleaning help my allergies?

Removing built-up dust, pollen, and pet dander from your ducts reduces how much of it gets recirculated through your home. For the best results, pair it with a quality filter that's changed on schedule.

Can you remove mold from my ducts?

Cleaning removes debris and our sanitizing treatment helps with odors, but growth will come back unless the moisture source is fixed. If we see signs of moisture, we'll show you and recommend next steps, including professional testing or HVAC repair when needed.

Are your sanitizing products safe for pets?

We use EPA-registered products applied per label directions and will explain the product and any re-entry time before we start. Sanitizing is optional.

Should I change my air filter after a duct cleaning?

Yes. Put in a fresh filter after the cleaning, and check it again a few weeks later, especially after construction cleanup.
